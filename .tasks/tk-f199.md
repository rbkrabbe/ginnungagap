## Context

Found while reviewing tk-3cd2 (NotLeader gained `leader_id`). Pre-existing gap,
not caused by that change, so it was left alone there.

docs/ggap-server.md:52 maps `NotLeader` to `UNAVAILABLE` "(with leader hint in
message)". That is doubly imprecise: the hint is not in the message, it is in
gRPC metadata, and the doc names neither key. `ggap_to_status` emits
`ggap-leader-id` and `ggap-leader-addr` as separate metadata entries, either of
which may be absent. A client author reading only this table would not know to
look at metadata at all.

The same table documents `WrongShard` without naming `ggap-shard-id`, which
`convert.rs` also emits — worth fixing in the same pass.

## Acceptance

- [ ] The `NotLeader` row names both metadata keys and says they are metadata,
      not message text, and that either may be absent.
- [ ] The `WrongShard` row names `ggap-shard-id`.
- [ ] Wording agrees with docs/ggap-types.md and docs/ggap-consensus.md on which
      half of the leader hint is stable and which may be stale.
