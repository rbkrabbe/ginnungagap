## Context

Independent of the `Node` change — carries no openraft edit and can land first.

`--client-addr` is a bind address (`0.0.0.0:17000`) with no advertised
counterpart, while `--cluster-addr` is advertised with `--cluster-listen-addr`
as its bind (main.rs:37-44). That asymmetry is the only reason
`derive_client_addr` exists. Give the client listener the same pair and the
derivation has nothing left to do.

- `main.rs` — `--client-addr` becomes advertised; add `--client-listen-addr`,
  defaulting to the resolved `--client-addr` exactly as `cluster_listen_addr`
  does at main.rs:171-179.
- `gossip.rs` — delete `derive_client_addr`, `split_host_port`, `host_of`,
  `port_of` and their 7 unit tests; the self-seed uses the configured advertised
  address directly.
- `deploy/k8s/ggap/configmap.yaml:23` — `--client-addr` must become the
  advertised name, with `--client-listen-addr 0.0.0.0:17000` alongside. A deploy
  left unchanged would advertise `0.0.0.0:17000` to the whole cluster, so this is
  the one place where getting it wrong is silent rather than loud.
- Test harnesses construct addresses directly and are unaffected beyond the
  `GossipTask::new` argument.

## Implementation notes

The derivation had 5 unit tests, not the 7 the plan estimated; all are gone
along with `derive_client_addr`, `host_of`, `port_of`, `split_host_port` and
the `ggap_consensus` re-export.

`--client-listen-addr` mirrors `--cluster-listen-addr` exactly: an explicit
value parses as a `SocketAddr`, and omitting it resolves `--client-addr`
through `lookup_host`. That resolution is what keeps the defaults
(`0.0.0.0:17000`) and the README's single-node invocations working unchanged —
a wildcard or loopback advertised address still resolves to itself.

The ConfigMap keeps relying on the `--cluster-listen-addr` default: the pod's
own DNS name resolves to its pod IP, which binds fine. Only `--client-addr`
needed the explicit bind override added, since it now names a host that is not
what the listener should bind. Adding `--cluster-listen-addr` alongside it was
considered and left out as out-of-scope churn.

`deploy/k8s/bootstrap/job.yaml` carried a comment describing port 17000 as "the
advertised form of the `--client-addr 0.0.0.0:17000` bind" — no longer true, so
it now just says the payload must match what each pod advertises. The addresses
the Job sends were already correct and are unchanged.
