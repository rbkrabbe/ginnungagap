## Context

Scope limits were s=2 / m=6 / l=15. Raised to 4 / 9 / 22.

The old numbers mis-sized a specific and recurring shape of change: one signature or type change rippling through every call site. tk-c507 hit it — conceptually one idea (carry a second address through the gossip layer), but changing `ShardRegistry::new` broke every construction site in the workspace, landing at 12 files. It was filed `m`, tripped the hook at 7, and had to be resized to `l` mid-implementation, which forced a spec approval in the middle of the work rather than before it. No scope had been absorbed; the label was just wrong for the shape.

The new numbers keep the limits meaningful — a genuinely sprawling change still trips them — while letting a mechanical ripple stay the size it feels like. They also mean tk-441f (flipping openraft's Node type, 19 files) fits `l` instead of breaching it, which would otherwise have forced a split whose only purpose was satisfying the counter.

Task files do not count: `is_bookkeeping` excludes all of `.tasks/`.
